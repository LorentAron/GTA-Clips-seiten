GTA Clips Uploader

A personal publishing tool for short gaming clips.

GTA Clips Uploader is a small private tool run by a single content creator. It takes short vertical gaming clips (Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay highlights) that the creator has edited and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts on a fixed schedule, using the official TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in once with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants the video.publish permission.
  2. Finished clips are placed in the tool's clip folder together with a title and hashtags.
  3. The tool uploads one clip at a time, at least two hours apart, to the creator's own account.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not post on behalf of other users.
